Publications

Our research has been published in leading scientific journals. Below is a selection of recent publications from the Nussear Lab.

2024

Nussear, K.E., Author, A., & Author, B.

Climate-driven changes in desert tortoise habitat suitability in the Mojave Desert

Ecological Applications, 34(2), 123-145.

Abstract: This study examines how projected climate changes will affect habitat suitability for the desert tortoise (Gopherus agassizii) across the Mojave Desert. Using species distribution models and climate projections, we identify areas of predicted habitat loss and potential refugia under different climate scenarios.

View Publication

Student, J., Nussear, K.E., & Collaborator, C.

Spatial patterns of wildlife connectivity in fragmented landscapes

Landscape Ecology, 39(4), 567-589.

Abstract: We quantify landscape connectivity for multiple wildlife species in the Great Basin using circuit theory and movement data. Our findings reveal critical corridors for wildlife movement and highlight areas where habitat fragmentation poses the greatest threat to population connectivity.

View Publication

2023

Nussear, K.E., Researcher, D., & Author, E.

Effects of drought on reptile population dynamics in arid ecosystems

Journal of Ecology, 111(8), 1234-1250.

Abstract: Long-term demographic studies reveal how extreme drought events affect reptile populations in desert ecosystems. We document changes in survival rates, reproduction, and population structure during multi-year droughts and discuss implications for conservation management.

View Publication

Graduate, M., Nussear, K.E., & Partner, F.

Integrating remote sensing and field data for habitat assessment

Remote Sensing of Environment, 285, 113401.

Abstract: We develop an integrated approach combining satellite imagery, drone-based remote sensing, and ground-truth field data to assess habitat quality across large spatial scales. This methodology improves accuracy of habitat models and enables more effective monitoring of habitat changes.

View Publication

2022

Nussear, K.E., Coauthor, G., Coauthor, H., & Coauthor, I.

Conservation planning for climate change: lessons from desert ecosystems

Conservation Biology, 36(5), e13901.

Abstract: Based on two decades of research in desert ecosystems, we present a framework for conservation planning that explicitly incorporates climate change projections. We discuss strategies for identifying climate refugia, managing for resilience, and prioritizing conservation actions in the face of uncertainty.
