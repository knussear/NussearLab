News & Updates

January 15, 2025

New Research Grant Awarded

The Nussear Lab has been awarded a new research grant to study wildlife habitat connectivity in the Great Basin. This three-year project will examine how landscape features affect species movement and gene flow, with implications for conservation corridor design.

December 8, 2024

New Publication in Conservation Biology

Our latest research on climate change impacts has been published in Conservation Biology. The study presents new findings on how temperature changes affect species distributions in arid ecosystems. Read more

November 20, 2024

Lab Member Presents at National Conference

Graduate student presented research findings at the National Wildlife Conference. The presentation highlighted our work on species distribution modeling and received excellent feedback from the conservation community.

October 5, 2024

Welcome New Graduate Student

We are excited to welcome a new graduate student to the lab. They will be working on habitat modeling for endangered species. Welcome to the team!

September 12, 2024

Field Season Success

The summer field season has concluded with excellent data collection. Our team successfully surveyed multiple sites and gathered crucial information for ongoing research projects. Thanks to all the field crew members for their hard work!
