Welcome

Welcome to the Nussear Lab at the University of Nevada, Reno. Our research focuses on ecology and wildlife conservation, with emphasis on understanding how species respond to environmental change and developing effective conservation strategies.

We combine field studies, spatial analysis, and modeling to address critical questions in conservation biology and inform evidence-based management decisions.
