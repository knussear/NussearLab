Welcome to the Nussear Lab

The Nussear Lab at the University of Nevada, Reno is dedicated to advancing research in ecology, conservation biology, and wildlife management. Our interdisciplinary team works on cutting-edge projects to understand and protect ecosystems and species.

Research Areas

Conservation Biology

Investigating strategies to preserve biodiversity and protect endangered species in changing environments.

Ecology

Studying the interactions between organisms and their environment to understand ecosystem dynamics.

Wildlife Management

Developing sustainable approaches to manage wildlife populations and their habitats.

About the Lab

Our laboratory combines field research, spatial analysis, and ecological modeling to address pressing conservation challenges. We collaborate with government agencies, non-profit organizations, and academic institutions to translate our research into actionable conservation strategies.
