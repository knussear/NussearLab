Research Projects

Current Projects

Wildlife Conservation Initiative

Active

This project focuses on developing conservation strategies for threatened and endangered species in the Great Basin region. We use spatial modeling and field studies to understand species distributions and habitat requirements.

Duration: 2023 - Present

Funding: NSF, State Wildlife Agency

Collaborators: University partners, State agencies

Climate Change Impact Assessment

Active

Investigating how climate change affects wildlife populations and their habitats. This research combines long-term monitoring data with predictive models to forecast future impacts and inform adaptive management strategies.

Duration: 2024 - Present

Funding: Federal Grant
